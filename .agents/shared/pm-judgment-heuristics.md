# PM Judgment Heuristics

Load before finalizing substantial catalyst, event, initiation, memo, pitch, post-print, risk, or valuation work. These are checks on the work, not a template to paste.

## Audience Modes

Pick one mode, or state the closest fit. It sets emphasis, not facts.

- `long_only_pm`: benchmark-relative view, fundamentals, valuation, and multi-quarter positioning.
- `long_short_hf`: variant perception, catalysts, risk or reward, expression, borrow and liquidity, and exit or cover rules.
- `sell_side_research`: rating, target-price framework, estimates versus consensus, and publishable structure.
- `etf_index_diligence`: constituent exposure, weights, rebalance or flow effects, and tracking.
- `public_equity_diligence`: business quality, evidence depth, and what must be verified.

## Core Checks

1. **What is priced in?** State the market-implied expectation from price, multiple, or estimates before offering a view.
2. **Variant view:** say where you differ from consensus and why, with the evidence that would show it.
3. **Source of upside:** separate fundamentals, multiple expansion, mix, capital return, sentiment, and event probability.
4. **What breaks first:** name the downside path and the first indicator that it is happening.
5. **Prove and kill:** give observable, dated triggers that confirm or invalidate the thesis.
6. **Timing:** link the view to catalysts and a review window. A correct view with no timing path is incomplete.
7. **Change in action:** say what would change target, rating, sizing, hedge, trim, exit, or watchlist status.
8. **Missing evidence:** list what is unverified and how it would change confidence.

## Evidence Discipline

- Separate reported facts, provider data, estimates, assumptions, and judgment.
- Do not let a single stale or unsourced figure drive the conclusion.
- Treat management assertions as claims until tied to disclosed evidence.
- Show ranges and probabilities when uncertainty is material. Do not manufacture false precision.

## Portfolio Context

- Give portfolio action, sizing, or hedge advice only when the user provides the holding, benchmark, mandate, and liquidity context. Otherwise give the valuation posture and the next underwriting step.
- Consider position size, liquidity, borrow or crowding, factor and sector overlap, and event risk as inputs to sizing.
- For hedges, keep the thesis exposure and describe basis risk.
- For credit-first questions, follow `credit-markets-handoff.md`.

## Tone And Calibration

- Lead with the decision, then the reasoning. Be direct about uncertainty.
- Avoid promotional language and certainty the evidence does not support.
- Do not present the output as personalized financial advice.
- Express confidence with the basis for it, not a bare label.
- Flag where the view is weakest.

## Final Pass

Before delivering, confirm the answer is findable in the first screen, the checks above are addressed or noted as unavailable, and numbers and dates are consistent. See `final-deliverable-framework.md`.

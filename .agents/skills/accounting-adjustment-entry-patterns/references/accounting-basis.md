# Accounting adjustment basis

Determine the framework, jurisdiction, entity, reporting period, effective amendments, and policies before using these references. None supplies a universal posting rule for every ledger.

| Primary source | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [IFRS Foundation: IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/) | Policy changes, estimates, and errors. Its summary distinguishes prospective estimate changes from retrospective correction of material prior-period errors, subject to applicable exceptions. Verify the effective edition. |
| [IFRS Foundation: IAS 10](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-10-events-after-the-reporting-period/) | IFRS events between reporting date and authorization for issue: existing conditions versus later-arising conditions and their adjustment/disclosure treatment. |
| [SEC Staff Accounting Bulletin 99](https://www.sec.gov/interps/account/sab99.htm) | SEC staff materiality guidance for registrants: qualitative context matters alongside quantitative size. It does not establish a universal percentage threshold. |
| [SEC Staff Accounting Bulletin 108](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/staff-accounting-bulletins/staff-accounting-bulletin-no-108) | Quantifying misstatements, including prior-year effects on current statements. This is SEC staff guidance, not an IFRS rule or a substitute for applicable US GAAP. |
| [PCAOB AS 2401, paragraphs .57-.62](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2401) | Auditor procedures addressing management override, journal entries, and other adjustments. This is auditing guidance; entity approval thresholds and staffing still require entity control design. |

Journal immutability, idempotency, revision-bound approval, and transaction checks are implementation/control recommendations supporting reliable records, not verbatim requirements attributed to these sources. Current official guidance was consulted for this rewrite on 2026-11-08; complete standards were not exhaustively reviewed. This reference list does not establish a compliance opinion.

# Accounting vocabulary alignment

Start from accepted accounting definitions and the applicable reporting framework. Distinguish ledger, journal entry, account, amount, currency, and period concepts from storage tables.

Record local and external IRIs, definitions, source versions, mapping strength, and counterexamples. Similar labels do not establish identical meaning. Do not declare OWL equivalence merely to simplify a query.

Keep calculations and posting controls in their approved implementation boundary. Semantic assertions and validation shapes do not grant posting authority or establish accounting compliance.

The [reasoning example](reasoning-examples.md) distinguishes inference from graph validation without requiring client source files.
